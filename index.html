Before you press order.

Short answers to what people ask the studio most. Anything else, ask a person below.

Does Hushkey need its own hub?

No. It joins the hub your home already has over the open smart-home standards, and it keeps working on its own when the internet drops.

How long does the battery last?

About two years of everyday pressing. It charges over USB-C in an afternoon, and the keys keep their positions while it does.

Can I change what each key does?

Yes, once, on a setup page on your phone. After that the panel works without the app — that is rather the point.

How do chords work?

Hold any two keys for a second and Hushkey saves what the room is doing as a chord. Press the same pair again and the room returns to it.

How does it mount?

The woven back holds to smooth walls, glass and tile. Lift from the bottom edge to move it: no screws, no marks, no adhesive.

When does it ship?

The first Chalk and Graphite panels ship in March. Pre-orders are charged only when your panel leaves the studio.
